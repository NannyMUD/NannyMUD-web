NannyMUD is a text game you play over the internet with a MUD client. It is free, and you need nothing but a client and the address:

mud.lysator.liu.se   port 2000

First steps

  1. Open your client and connect to mud.lysator.liu.se, port 2000.
  2. Type a name to make a new character, or guest to look around first.
  3. In the game, type help newbie. The same pages are on this site, and so is the Newbie Booklet.

Clients

A MUD client keeps what you type apart from what the game sends, remembers your commands, and lets you write aliases and triggers. Any of these will do:

ClientRuns onGood for
MudletWindows, macOS, LinuxThe usual choice today: a window per channel, maps, and scripting in Lua.
TinTin++Linux, macOS, WindowsPlaying in a terminal, also over ssh from another machine. Its own scripting language.
BlightmudLinux, macOSA terminal client scripted in Lua.
MUSHclientWindowsA long-standing Windows client with plugins in Lua.

Plain telnet

Without a client, telnet works too:

telnet mud.lysator.liu.se 2000

On Windows, telnet has to be turned on first under Windows Features ("Telnet Client"). Recent macOS no longer ships it; nc mud.lysator.liu.se 2000 works instead. On Linux it is in the telnet package.
