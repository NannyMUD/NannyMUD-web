NannyMUD's own scripts and libraries for Mudlet, shared by players for players. They read the data the mud sends over GMCP, so your health, your foe, your party, the room and your guild can sit in their own windows instead of being read off the screen.

RepositoryNannyMUD/NannyMUD-mudlet

Getting started

  1. In Mudlet, open Settings, then Game protocols, and tick Enable GMCP. Reconnect.
  2. In the game, type toggle gmcp on. toggle gmcp lists what the mud can send, and toggle gmcp <name> on turns each part on.
  3. Download a package from the repository and install it in Mudlet with Toolbox, then Package Manager, then Install. Its own page in the repository says what it shows and how to set it up.

What the mud sends

Each part is off until you turn it on, and the mud sends it only when your client asks for it.

Turned on withWhat is in it
toggle gmcp vitalsHit points, spell points, and what you carry.
Char.Vitals
toggle gmcp foeWhom you are fighting and how hurt they are.
Char.Foe
toggle gmcp statusWhat score shows: level, experience, money and the rest.
Char.Status
toggle gmcp groupYour party, as party status shows it.
Group.Info
toggle gmcp roomThe room you are in and its exits, for a mapper.
Room.Info
toggle gmcp channelsChannel lines, for a chat window.
Comm.Channel.Text
toggle gmcp discordYour status for Discord, if Mudlet shows it there.
External.Discord
toggle <guild> gmcpYour guild's own state, for members. In the game, help in your guild says what is in it.
Guild.Alchemy, Guild.Strigoi, Guild.Druid
