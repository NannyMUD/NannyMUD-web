NannyMUD's own scripts for TinTin++, shared by players for players. They read the data the mud sends over GMCP and keep it in a panel on the right of the screen, with a box at the top for tells and channels, and they keep the mud's line width in step when you resize the window.

RepositoryNannyMUD/NannyMUD-tintin

The files

FileWhat it does
nanny.tinStart here. Reads the others and connects.
nanny.split.tinThe message box at the top. When the window changes size it is drawn again and the mud is told the new size, so it wraps to fit.
nanny.gmcp.tinThe panel on the right: your health, your foe, the room, your score and your party. Tells and channels go to the top box.
nanny.gmcp.alchemy.tinThe Alchemists' part of the panel: minions, stores and concoctions.
nanny.gmcp.strigoi.tinThe Strigoi part: level, form, damage and what is on you.
nanny.gmcp.druid.tinThe Druids' part: pets, barkskin, staff and wand.

Getting started

  1. Download the files into one folder and start TinTin++ there with tt++ nanny.tin.
  2. If your guild has a file, take the #nop off the line in nanny.tin that reads it.
  3. Log in and type gmcp_on once. It turns GMCP on in the game, every part of it, and the mud remembers. For your guild's part, also toggle <guild> gmcp on.

The panel shows when the window is at least 110 columns wide.
