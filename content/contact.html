If you have a question, please consider this first:

  • Please check the FAQ first; it's possible that your question is answered there.
  • If you have a question that's not included in the FAQ, please phrase it in such a way that we may include it!
  • Most questions are often best answered inside the game. Your fellow players are excellent resources and may know more than you think.
  • In fact, we recommend that you log on to the game to contact anyone. There are several options to contact any administrator within the game, including shouting, telling, and mailing. In the post office, you can do 'mail admin' to reach all of the administration.

Having said that, if you still have an important question for the select members of the NannyMUD administration that read emails, please mail them at nannymud@lysator.liu.se. Do not expect quick replies.
