Hello and welcome to the Guild of Prophets. So, what is this Prophet guild all about? It's all about predicting what will happen next.

The guild was once initiated by Nostradamus, and is now a relic in the backwaters of other, more powerful guilds. But don't despair: it is written that Prophets have yet to see their greatest days. You can find the guild in Profezzorn's area. Type join prophets in the guild hall to become one.

A prophet's ways

  • Advancement is automatic. Prophets grow by successfully using their prophetic powers, and slowly by growing older and wiser.
  • Prophets are not very sociable people. They prefer to be by themselves most of the time, and so they have no guild line. Other Prophets are not interested in what you have to say.
  • As a prophet you are a generous individual. Now and then you may find yourself donating money to living beings around you who are not so well off as yourself.
  • Prophets are not as effective at melee as some other guilds, and prefer light weapons and light armour.

Armageddon and Crashmas

Armageddon is a time of great rejoicing for the Prophets. All your work has basically led you to this point. Since the world is about to end, it is only fair that you are allowed to enjoy the last moments of your existence, and most of your powers work much, much better.

Since Armageddon is a rare event, the Prophets celebrate the anniversary of the last twenty of them. The anniversary is called Crashmas, and a calendar on the wall in the guild halls shows when the next one will take place.

From wannabe to meteorologist

The prophet wannabe rises through the oracle wannabe, the doubtful psychic, the fortuneteller, the astrologer and the oracle to the prophet and the master prophet, and finally to the meteorologist.
