The Druids Guild is a guild for the friends of Trees and the worshippers of the Wonders of Nature. The symbols of the link between the Druid and the Nature is the Tree, the Owl and the Wooden Staff.

Druids Guild is a Guild of useful powers and thematic additions like feelings and other social skills. The guild is therefore a good guild for newcomers to the game. But it is also enjoyable for more experienced players since the powers of the Nature grows stronger as your knowledge increases.

The Druids survive as a guild through a combination of power, wits and co-operation with the Nature and fellow druids. They are observant on how light comes and goes in Nature and they feel especially comfortable in the forest where they are surrounded by trees.

Joining

A new druid is brought in by another. Talk to a Druid and they will help you to join.

The power trees

Druids grow through the power trees, from the Birch through the Rowan, Ash, Alder, Willow, Oak and Hazel to the Elder, by using their druidic powers wisely. A druid who uses them unwisely may find their tree abandons them. Each druid chooses their own title, and it begins with the letter of their tree.

The Tree and the Owl

Druids can create trees in most places. The trees are the link between this world and the spiritual world. Inside their own tree a Druid can sleep to regain lost strength, or meditate to be carried to the spiritual world where the guild chambers are.

As a special gift from the forest, a Druid can summon an owl that will keep them company during their travels. The owl can also be a source of information and it will obey any commands given.

The way of the forest

The powers of a druid are stronger in the forest and on the druid holidays. Metal does not sit well with them, and a druid carrying it finds their powers weakened.
