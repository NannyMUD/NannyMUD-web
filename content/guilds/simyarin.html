Simyarin is the gathering of magical users in NannyMUD. It has several different schools, so it can suit any type of player, and it allows a greater degree of personalization than any other guild. It is rather difficult to play.

Simyar-Lome

The guild lies on an island far to the west, past the orc valley, through a mountain and over the western sea. Knock on Dak Simyar, the gate of magic, to ask to be let in.

The schools

Once a Simyarin has grown in grade, they become apprentice to a master in one of the schools: Telemetry, the School of Teleporting; Chaos, the School of Chaos; and the Enchanters, the School of Enchantment. No mind of today can learn all the spells, not even of a single school, and so every Simyarin grows into their own personal characteristics.

The vow of secrecy

The Simyarin powers are magical and ancient. This knowledge is not common or public. By joining the Simyarin you take a vow of secrecy: you may not discuss spells or powers with anyone at all, nor ask others to reveal theirs. You may reveal your powers only by using them in front of others. A Simyarin may tell you where the guild is, but nothing about specific spells, and neither will this page.

Reverence

The Simyarin are a proud guild. A Simyarin should always behave with respect towards any higher Simyarin, especially the Simyarin nobles and their master.

Equipment

Simyarin do not normally use weapons or heavy armour, nor are they very good at making use of such items. It is not illegal to do so, but it is frowned upon by fellow Simyarin. Magical or light armour such as rings, cloaks and amulets are used more often.

Leaving

A school, once joined, can never be left. A Simyarin who leaves the guild itself is stripped of all magical powers, and will never be allowed to join again.
