The Order of the White Lily is a monastic order dedicated to the worship of the great god Lars, creator of the world. The order reveres that which is holy, and is for those who choose the path of good and righteousness.

" ...and Lars set_light(1)..."

-- quoted from an ancient text describing the origins of the universe

The Monastery

The White Lily Monastery stands within the walls of Basalt Keep on the continent of Antharis . The monastery contains a chapel, a garden, and a dormitory so that monks may begin each day's journey from within its walls. The old monastery still stands near Larstown, but its gardens have grown as many weeds as herbs in the days since the order left for the new world.

Mass

Several times a day a venerable monk of the order enters the chapel and performs a mass. The origins of the lessons are lost to memory, but represent a broad range of spiritual teachings.

It is required of members of the order that they attend each mass, although it is allowed to attend either at Basalt Keep or Larstown. Visitors to the mass are welcome so long as they respect the silence of the ceremony and the sanctity of the chapel. It is said that the healing touch of Lars visits those who attend mass in the Basalt Keep monastery chapel.

Healing

Lars grants his followers the ability to heal both the mind and body. For this reason, monks are regarded as valuable members in adventuring parties. The stronger a monk's faith in Lars, the more potent Lars' touch will be.

Herbalism

Herbalism is a significant aspect of a monk's duties. Herbs are a key component in much of the healing performed by monks. Since moving to Antharis, the old monk Hieronymous has become wise in the ways of the local flora and fauna. He has written at length about the particular healing and protective powers of some of the exotic plants that can be found (with a little persistance) in the varied climates of Antharis.

Mendel, the garden master, spends his time tending to the old world herbs used in the healing rituals. In his spare time he is known to experiment with peas...

The White Lily

In the chapel of the monastery you will find a depiction of Lars reaching down from the heavens and planting the flower of life upon the newly created world. The flower, of course, is a white lily. For the monks of the Order of the White Lily, the flower is a symbol of peace and purity.
