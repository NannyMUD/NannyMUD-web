The Viking clan of NannyMUD is a mighty band of warriors, strong in faith and fierce in battle. Here you can learn about their beginnings and history, the gods they worship, the warriors that lead them, and the ways in which you could become one of them.

The Beginning

In the beginning, before any of the world as we know it existed, the spring Hvergelmir gave rise to eleven rivers known as the Elivagar. Over a long period of time, water from the Elivagar poured into the endless void called Ginnungagap. The water froze and formed vast sheets of ice in the void. On each end of Ginnungagap were the two opposites: ice and fire, Niflheim and Muspell. Muspell melted some of the ice created, and created a zone of meltwater amid the ice and snow. Here life began, and here started the chain of Gods known as the Norse Gods.

The followers of the All-Father Odin and his sons and daughters were great seafarers, and explored all corners of the world in their famous longboats. The revered Erik the Stout sailed farther and wider than any other Viking, and came to an island southwest of Antharis. The Vikings named the island Hemgaard, and their home in Nanny is here today. Even though the Vikings of Hemgaard rarely use their longboats anymore, they are still great seafarers and they still call themselves "Vikings."

The Vikings are a tribe, a family, and are therefore naturally very loyal to each other. When they stand united against an enemy, they are very hard to defeat.

The Vikings all live together in a small village, which is where they perform their rites, meet and advance.

The Gods

The Vikings are followers of the Norse gods, and it is from their gods, and from each other, that they collect their strength. They show their respect to their gods through the rites in their honour, and by making sacrifices to please them. The Vikings have a very personal relationship to the gods, and are often called by the gods themselves to perform certain acts.

First of the Gods stands the All-Father, Odin (also known as "Wotan"), who sacrificed one of his eyes for one sip out of the Well of Wisdom; who sacrificed himself by hanging himself from Yggdrasil -- the world tree -- for nine days, pierced by his own spear. Through these sacrifices he gained tremendous knowledge.

Odin is the father of the gods; of Thor (the god of thunder), Balder (the god of light), and of Freya (the goddess of fertility and love). Odin's home in Asgard, where all the gods live, is known as Valaskjalf, "shelf of the slain." Here he sits on his throne Hlidskjalf and observes what happens in the nine worlds. The tidings are brought to him by his two ravens Huginn and Muninn.

The Vikings of Nanny choose to worship individual gods of the Norse Pantheon. Those who worship and honour Odin may share in his powers of wisdom and divination, while those who follow Thor feel the energy of his lightning fury. Baldur's worshippers bring light and blessing into the world, while Freya's followers nurture and fiercely protect their Viking brethren. The Viking will choose which god or gods to follow after gaining experience and thinking deeply about the direction of his life. But once the Viking chooses to follow a god, the burden of keeping the god's favour will forever lie on the viking's shoulders.

Our Leaders

The Viking clan of NannyMUD is run be several wise an revered leaders; men and women who have worshipped the Norse gods and bask high in their favour. Our Guild Masters are:

Boyd, the Hammer of Thor

Yaleah, the Daughter of Freya

Slakktor, the Eye of Odin

The Viking village also contains many elders who have perfected their own arts and skills and volunteer their time to teach those skills to the younger vikings. Their homes on Hemgaard are always open for their students.

Joining and Leaving

In order to join the family of Vikings on NannyMUD, you must find Ture the Boatman on the southwest shores of Antharis. He keeps his ship on a beach to the south and west of the realms of Balin and Yaleah. If you tell him that you would like to join the vikings, he will help you in any way he can.

If you ever choose to leave the Vikings, no harm will come to you, but all will be sad to see you go. Return your ring, the symbol of Viking brotherhood, to Eric the Stout, and he will transport you off the island.
