The lepers of Nanny are despised for their disease and therefore they often have to team up in order to survive. They are more fragile than people from other guilds. The Leper Colony is an intermediate guild to play.

The colony

The Lepers are not a guild in the regular sense, but rather a group of people who share a common trait. Due to both the social and physical stigmas attached to their debilitating condition, they tend to gather in loose-knit colonies and only associate with their own kind. Their main issue is survival and main creed safety in numbers. Thus is the Leper Colony formed.

This particular colony was the first of such communities. They have learned to use their disease to their advantage, the secrets passed from generation to generation. Until now they have dared not venture forth into the world. However, they now realize the potential threat of the outsiders. The ignorance and fear of the outside world could endanger the safety of the colony. Thus expansion must occur. Most lepers remain in the colony in relative peace. It is only those who have been passed the learned secrets of leprosy who can summon the strength to leave it. These same few also know of a cure, but it is rumoured that no one has sought the cure in decades.

Joining and leaving

Nobody joins the colony. You are infected. A leper far enough gone to be contagious can pass the disease on, and then you are one of them.

The nurse can cure leprosy. Once cured you can never rejoin: the cure lasts a lifetime.

The price of the disease

Being a leper isn't all glory. You are sick, remember. As your body decays your constitution suffers, you cannot heal fully by normal means, and monsters sometimes strike the parts of you that are more decayed than others. Once you are contagious, any non-leper in your party is affected by your close presence. Your skin grows too sensitive for heavy armour, and whatever you wield or wear becomes tainted, so that no non-leper will touch it.

The Leper Messiah

The leper with the most Prestige is raised to Leper Messiah, and granted powers beyond any ordinary leper's capacity.

Among their own

Being an outcast from normal society, a leper has only fellow colony members to rely on. Lepers shouldn't kill each other unprovoked. It is rumoured that repeat offenders have been forcibly cured of their sickness and thrown out from the colony. Members of any other guild are fair game.
