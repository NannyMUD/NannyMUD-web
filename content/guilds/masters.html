The Guild of Masters is for those players who find the game too easy, not challenging enough. It is designed to be hard to play, and it gets harder the higher you climb. Becoming a wizard in this guild is a true achievement. The guild motto is: Adapt, Improvise, Overcome.

No powers whatsoever

We all fail at times, and the risk thereof adds spice and challenge to our daily struggles. There is nothing as dull as the ensured success. The Masters have no powers at all; they master the game through their own will and skill.

At times, the members of the guild will decide to skip some actions. This is based in their conviction that they are the very best; they can ignore things and still master the game. This tendency to just ignore things gets stronger the higher they climb in the guild.

Outlook

The Masters of NannyMUD are safe and sure in their knowledge that they are the very best, and can thus at times be perceived as a little haughty and arrogant. And they have the right to be, being the very best.

Joining

Find the guild hall and type join masters. Only the young may begin: a character of level 5 or below, with no other guild. The mark of a Master is a golden star set with a large ruby.

History

In the beginning, the world was without form. Then Lars the Implementor created the Foundation of Worlds from the void, shaping it with just his intellect. On this foundation, he built the First World, Genesis.

A bit later, that world was visited by a small God named Mats. He looked at the First World, and decided to create one of his own. His creation was, in the end, named NannyMUD. Therein lies a mystery and the soul of this world; what would it have been if the name BrutalMUD had persevered?

Rather soon after the world was created, Brom started to attend it. He became a Wizard, and then a High Wizard, and then an Arch Wizard. During that time, the world grew, and so did the powers of the mortals. That was not entirely a good thing. The mortals were pampered by their guilds in such a way that they lost the cutting edge of skill, and instead became lazy script weasels. Gone were the days of challenge and sweet victory.

Then Brom decided to create a guild that would encourage the members to develop their skill, and their grip on the game. He named it the Guild of Masters: a guild to master the game, through your own will and skill.
