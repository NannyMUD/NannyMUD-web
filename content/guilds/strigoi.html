The strigoi are the undying of the old country. They take their own young, and once the blood binds there is no way out. What the guild gives back is a soul that does the remembering, and a long night ahead.

Joining

Only the young are taken: a character of the first levels, before the world has had its say. Go through the dark temple to the Abyssal Chamber, stand before the stone altar and say join strigoi. The walls remember. They do not forget.

Shapes

A strigoi has no fixed drawbacks. They pick them. Every shape gives in one place and takes in another, and a strigoi changes shape whenever the fight calls for it. Human form gives nothing and costs nothing. The rarer shapes open up as a strigoi rises.

Powers

Four kinds: spells, skills, curses and buffs. Spells fail where magic is forbidden. Skills do not care. Curses go on the enemy, buffs go on yourself. Every power keeps growing for as long as its owner does.

The halls

Past the chamber lie the Shadowed Hall, the Forbidden Archive and the Council Chamber, and a candlelit sanctum draped in crimson where the guild trades. Outsiders are turned back at the door.
