The kindred roam the world of Nanny in their constant search for fresh blood. They prefer the night, and welcome darkness. It is a hard guild to play in.

Lilith, first-born

The mother of all kindred is Lilith, first-born, eternal. She was the first vampire, and she shall be the last. From her, all other kindred descend. How she became a vampire, no one really knows. The childer of Lilith created their own childer, becoming Sires in their own right, and so the gift of the blood has been passed on down the eons, from Sire to Childe. Kindred take pride in their ancestries: the closer to Lilith first-born, the more status.

The Kiss

Nobody asks to join the kindred. A vampire creates a childe from a mortal by first drawing all blood from it. Then, at the very brink of death, the vampire offers its own blood for the mortal to drink. Once a single drop is sampled, the lust for more will be uncontrollable. Thus a new childe has been created, and the Sire is responsible for teaching it the ways of the kindred, the need for secrecy, and the traditions.

The Motherhouse

Lilith rules all kindred from a mansion lying secluded in faraway silent hills, only visited by the cold winds of the north. There she sits on her night throne, rarely moving, always aware. The Motherhouse is sacred ground to kindred, and no act of violence is tolerated inside. Behind it lies a walled park where Lilith keeps prey for her childer in times of great need. Far away, an ancient tomb in the desert of the Wandering Sands on Antharis gives travelling kindred rest and shelter against the sun.

The Traditions

Respect
A childe should always honour and respect its Sire, and a younger vampire the knowledge, power and authority of its elders.
Tribute
Lilith claims half a vampire's income in tribute and divides it among the vampire's elders and herself. The elders naturally approve. The younger kindred sometimes mutter under their breath.
Progeny
A vampire learned in the ways of the kindred should Kiss those mortals it deems worthy of the gift of blood.
Modesty
But not too many, lest the blood become too thin and the power of the gift wasted.
Challenges
To prevent chaotic diablerie, a challenger goes to Lilith, and if she agrees, the duel ends with only one survivor.

Secrecy

The kindred keep their powers and their weaknesses to themselves, and spreading them to mortals is punished by banishment. This page will not tell you either.
