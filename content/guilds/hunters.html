As new lands are discovered, adventurous people seek out these new frontiers to find their fortunes. These hardy individuals bring little with them but their skills. They learn to kill the animals in the environment, skin them and sell the pelts for profit. They can make most of the things they need from available materials. These hunters live by their wits and skills. Unskilled hunters struggle to survive, but skilled hunters can make use of wood and parts from animals that they kill. As they learn the ways of the native wildlife, even the strongest creatures can be defeated.

The bowie knife

The Hunter's bowie knife is both the guild mark and the hunter's basic weapon and tool. With it a hunter skins the kills, cuts branches, and in time makes everything else: fur coats and leather jackets, moccasins and warbonnets, spears, longbows and arrows, nets, tents and waterbags.

Joining

Davy sells bowie knives at the Trading Post, the guild headquarters, for 1000 gold coins, and a knife is all it takes. A new hunter has a tough time getting started, which separates the rugged individualists from the wimps, so Davy only sells to the young: a character of level 3 or less, with little experience. Hunters live balanced lives, and Davy will not sell to anyone too good or too evil. Hunters aren't quitters either: anyone who leaves is never allowed back.

The hunter's life

  • Hunters use their own equipment for most things. Quest equipment may be used, but it is only a third as effective. Hunter equipment can't be used by other guilds.
  • Hunters function best with neutral alignment. As the alignment becomes unbalanced, hunter skills become less effective.
  • Hunters need to be fully alert to use their skills. Sober up before trying to fight or to make things.
  • A hunter grows skilled at hunting each kind of creature, and skills that are neglected decline.

The Head Hunter

The highest title that a mortal hunter can achieve is Head Hunter. Only one hunter can hold this title, so when another hunter achieves it, the previous one becomes a former Head Hunter.

History

The idea for the Hunters guild originated in January 1998 while Vincent was waiting for yet another quest to be approved. Vincent tried to make the guild accurately reflect the Mountain Man lifestyle, within the limits allowed by the NannyMUD guild rules, so no guns or other modern equipment. The guild opened on June 28, 1998. Due to an equipment shortage, only a few hunters were allowed to join at a time.
