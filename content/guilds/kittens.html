A strange bunch of mathematicians located northwards of Elm square. Expect to be, as we said... strange.

The Kitten and his Theory

There is a man called The Kitten. He has a theory, the Kitten Theory. The Kitten Theory will solve everything. Currently, if you add everything in the universe together, it should equal zero. It does not. If you add the correct Kitten Theory to everything else, it will come out as zero. Therefore, the correct Kitten Theory does exist as a force. What you do is you try to help him find the correct theory (there are thousands of mildly incorrect variants) and modify the theory, and therefore reality, to your own advantage.

You have to admire him for his dedication and hard work. Not many can keep working on a theory for so long. We all have to help him. And we all strive to be like him.

Questions often asked

Kittens. That's like baby cats, right?
No.
Where do I fit into that?
Well, you help him out. You contribute spell points to help him along, and it just so happens that if you tweak a few numbers here and there, some monsters have a tendency to get hurt.
Won't changing the numbers like that hurt The Kitten?
No. The correct theory will work with any set of numbers.
So it's just a theory?
It's a theory outlining a force, rather like the Theory of Gravity is.
Will The Kitten get mad if I don't capitalize his name?
He's got bigger things to worry about.
What happens if the theory gets to 100%?
One of two things. If this is the correct version of the theory, then something really big and good happens. No one is entirely sure what. If this is not the correct version, then it works for a little bit, then snaps.
Snaps?
Yeah. Something happens. To everyone in the guild.

Specialization

Sometimes The Kitten decides that the theory would work a bit better if certain creatures were dead. He declares that the world needs fewer goblins, say, or fewer creatures whose name begins with the letter N. It is suggested that you kill them, because if you don't, The Kitten will stop working, as he doesn't like being ignored. You get a rather nice combat bonus against these creatures.

Backlash

Changing universal constants is not a precise thing, nor is it a very nice thing at times. Using powers can cause backlash. You can't really control it, and it is usually bad.

Joining and leaving

The Kitten's Foyer lies north of Elm square. Read the scroll in the social room, and if you agree, join guild. The rules are few: don't bait someone else into breaking the rules, don't annoy the Kitten, don't kill other kittens, and don't steal kills.

To leave, go to the advancement room and type leave kittens. You lose all your guild levels and guild points.
