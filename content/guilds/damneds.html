The guild of the damned is a house of necromancers and the things they have stopped being. A member carries a soulgem, changes shape when it suits them, and answers to a hierarchy of members, priests, elders and founders, under Plaque the Lord of Darkness.

Becoming undead

To be a Damned you have to become an undead, and the joining ends with your death. Only the young are taken: a character of the first level, with no other guild, who has not wandered too far into the quests of the world. Come to the Entrance chambers deep in Radagim's caves as a ghost, read the rules on the wall, and apply. The High priest of the Damneds does the rest.

If you later want to leave the guild, you will also have to die, and get a new, normal body by praying in the church.

The way of the damned

As a Damned, you should always try to be as evil as possible, because the dark powers of undead work better the more evil the possessor of the powers is. A saint in this guild casts bolts that do no damage at all.

The powers feed on unholy power, which a damned gains by sacrificing experience at the obelisk. The higher they rise, the more they need.

Friends and enemies

The Guild has no objection whatsoever to playerkilling; it does not demand it nor prohibit it. An exception, though, is that we do not appreciate newbie slayers in the Guild.

The Damneds do not particularly like any members of other Guilds, but don't have any certain enemy guilds. An exception is the Monk guild, which every damned hates worse than anything, just because of the monks' usually horribly positive alignment.
