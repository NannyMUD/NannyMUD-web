The legendary Knights of Camelot are both skilled and noble warriors. Knights display courage and camaraderie in the face of frequent invasions. Knighthood is a challenging experience, yet a glorious one.

The knight guild is comprised of people who have come seeking the greater good, people who hold valour, honour, kindness, loyalty and respect dear. Knights are brave and daring, they stare death in the face. Knights are honest, they keep their word, which is as good as gold. The Assembly is a compassionate guild, willing to help others out: knights help newcomers find their way around Nanny.

The code of chivalry

Loyalty
Knights shall be known by their commitment to the ideals of the realm, service to the guild and their fellow knights.
Respect
Knights shall always show respect to their fellow knights, and any others they might meet. Regardless of what the other people might be, say or do, a knight is still a knight and must act accordingly.
Justice
A knight's sword is the path of right, without bias or personal interest, tempered by humanity, each fold hammered with mercy.
Humility
Knights know that virtue is earned. They never boast or glorify themselves.
Defense
Knights are sworn by oath to the defense of their guild, their realm and their fellow knights.
Courage
Knights stand in the face of adversity and take the side of truth, and hold the wisdom to know when to stand and when to retreat.

Joining

Go to Camelot, to the office of the Assembly, and apply to King Arthur. Only the good are taken: a knight must be above neutral, and should stay that way by ridding the world of evil. The Round Table, the council of the highest ranking knights, watches over who is let in.

Restrictions

  • As a knight you are denied magic.
  • Knights should always keep an alignment above neutral. The guild's powers weaken as a knight slips.
  • Knights hold the code of chivalry high.

Enemies

Besides all the enemies that attack Camelot, all servants of darkness are the knights' natural rivals, the Dark guild first among them. Knights treat their enemies with the courtesy of a knight, but never aid them.

Leaving

A knight may leave the guild at any time. One who returns starts from scratch, and the Round Table is not pleased to rejoin someone for little reason.
