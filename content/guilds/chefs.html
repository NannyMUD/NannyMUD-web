The Chefs' Guild is a gathering of adventurers who are all interested in food and cooking techniques. Wherever we go we try to find high-quality meat and prepare gourmet food from it. Sometimes we succeed and sometimes we learn a lesson. We always have our Chef's Kit with us and we wear our white Chef's Hat with pride.

Joining

Anyone can become a Chef. Just go to the Kitchen Entrance of Honeywheel Castle and talk to Henri, the Executive Chef, and he will help you.

The Chef's Hat and the Kit

The mark of a Chef is his white Chef's Hat that he wears with pride. The hat keeps track of the animals you know how to cook and what styles you have prepared. It also remembers how many Chef Credits you have earned and your status and title in the guild.

When the Chef logs on he goes to the Pantry and retrieves his Chef's Kit. In it he keeps his tools and the ingredients he buys in the Pantry. As he advances in the guild the number of tools in the Kit increases, as do the amount of spices and additionals he can carry around. There is also room for some jars of stored food.

The guild area

The Chefs Guild is based in Honeywheel Castle in Banshee's area. In the Kitchen Entrance you join the guild, advance in the guild and check the list of quests. Henri is always there to help you.

North of the Kitchen Entrance is the Kitchen, where more experienced Chefs can extend your knowledge about cooking. Of course, they are entitled to some compensation for their efforts. You pay your teacher with Chef Credits. There is one expert for each animal, and the experts don't work in the Kitchen every day. An example, if you want to get a lesson on how to cook frog meat, you should come here a day when Stuart, the frog Chef, is here.

Then there is the Pantry, where you retrieve your Chef's Kit before starting your travels. There you can also buy spices and additionals that you need to cook delicious food. The quality of the spices and the additionals varies during the year. Freshly harvested vegetables are usually better than the ones that have been stored all winter.

There is also a library where the members of the Chefs' Guild can read about the different creatures in the world; what kind of food they can give and where they usually can be found. There is also a board where you can brag about successful dishes and warn other Chefs about dangerous combinations of spices.

On the continent of Antharis the Chefs Guild has opened a second pantry. It is located in the back of the shack where the Road House is.

Rules

  • Before you can cook anything you have to retrieve your Chef's Kit from the Pantry.
  • Since you are carrying your kit around, you can't use weapons that require you to use both hands, and you can't use a shield and wield a weapon at the same time.
  • Since you always have to wear your Chef's Hat, you can't use helmets.
  • As a Chef you don't enjoy food that wasn't prepared by a Chef to its full value.
  • It is allowed to trade meat with other Chefs.
  • It is allowed to sell food to hungry players.

Leaving

When you think you have learnt everything there is to learn about food, you are free to leave Chefs' Guild, but you have to digest all the food in your stomach first.
