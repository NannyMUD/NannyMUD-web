The dark guild is an order of ascetic blademasters, who also hone their minds with the study of forbidden rituals. They have a clear and singular common cause: to extinguish the Sun, to let the beautiful world of Nanny be seen as it really should be, in darkness. Darkness is their friend and companion, the Sun their eternal enemy. In fact, all light is to be defeated at every opportunity. Work toward this end, in harmony with the brothers, sisters and others of the cause, and you shall become one with the void.

The guild hall

The guild is located inside the heart of the Backbone Mountains, north of the great plains that lie just west of the village. To find it, journey north across the plains to the entrance of a mine. In there, you will find a hole in the ground. Jump down there, and you will find your way to the guild. The rooms are, naturally, laid out in the shape of a pentacle. On Antharis there is a veil, in a cave system under the plains of Earthwound, that leads to the same halls.

Joining

Those who wish to join the Dark guild need to find two members who are already well along the path, and convince them to vote for them. With two such votes of trust secured, the aspiring worshipper of darkness must find an elder member to initiate them.

No goody-two-shoes are allowed into the guild. Before being initiated, the wannabe must be very evil, at least halfway on the road to being demonic. Otherwise, no one will initiate them.

The rules of the dark

  • You may only fight with blades. They are not mere weapons, but rather an extension of your warrior soul.
  • You may not use shields. Your training focuses on speed and balance and technique rather than crude force.
  • You prefer black or dark equipment, and white or unusually bright equipment truly bothers you.
  • Light is harmful, the Sun is the enemy. Light interferes with your link to the void.
  • You must be evil, as evil as you ever can be. A dark who turns good is suspended from the guild until they repent.

The Midnight Ritual

Every night at midnight, Nanny time, the guild members who are logged on gather at the Midnight Ritual. Here, the highest ranking member of the guild leads the others in a glorious celebration of darkness and the struggle to extinguish all light. Blades earned through sweat and blood are joined with the master sword of the guild, and sources of hateful light are banished forever.

Leaving

For those unfaithful souls who stray from the path to darkness, there is a way out. At sunrise, Nanny time, they can walk out into the open cave at the far north end of the guild. There they stand and await the coming of the fiery orb they once called enemy, and if they take the sight to heart and find it beautiful, they will be cleansed of all darkness and once more be free to choose their own path in life.
