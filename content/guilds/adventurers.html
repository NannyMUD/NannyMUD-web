The Adventurers' Guild is the guild for all new players that arrive to NannyMUD. The guild will help you understand the way of being a successful adventurer and prepare you for your future in other, more specialized guilds.

Every new character starts here. There is nothing to join.

What the guild gives you

Reading your guide, a guild title, and the realm command, which tells you who created the area you are visiting. As you grow, the guild teaches you a simple light spell and two basic combat spells, kick and stun.

The greatest gift of the guild is exploring. Adventurers who keep moving to new places are quietly rewarded for it.

Advice for a newbie

  • Read your Newbie Booklet right away, and use it as a source of reference until you know how to be a successful player. It is on this site too.
  • Explore the Mud. It can be dangerous in the beginning, but the rewards can be great and well worth your while.
  • Use Malbeet and the Tourist Office, south, east, east, south, south and east from the Church. Press the red button to summon Malbeet and he will take you on a tour of NannyMUD.

Moving on

When you have found the guild you want, leave guild and go and join it. Several guilds take only the youngest characters, so look around early.
