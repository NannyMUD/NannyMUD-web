North of the steep hills, at the head of a valley nobody bothered to name, a tower has been smoking for as long as anybody can remember. It belongs to Aademus, who is old, wears a fine old coat, and eats dates. He is looking for apprentices.

Who he takes

Fresh blood only: a character of level one, every stat at one, nothing learned, no quest solved and no other guild behind it. Find the tower by following the smoke, climb to Aademus, and say join alchemy.

Science, not magic

Nothing an alchemist does is magic, and an alchemist will tell you so at some length. It is all science, which has the great advantage of working in places where magic does not.

An alchemist fights with concoctions, brewed from earth, wind, water, metal and mercury and thrown at whatever needs it. Beside them come the skills of the trade, and a household of made things that grows as the apprentice does: a golem that digs, a clockwork that smelts, a serpent that brews, a brass camel that carries and shoots, a little man who shows the way, a mandrake that screams and a pelican that feeds its master.

The ladder

The way up has six tiers and five gates between them. Everything an alchemist does that hurts something, and everything extracted or brewed, counts toward the next step. Aademus takes a fee at every step and calls it tuition. Each gate passed brings a gift, and the longer an alchemist fights, the more streetwise they become.

The Order

Some alchemists kill other players. They are the Order, they say so in their title.

Leaving

An alchemist who has had enough says so twice in Aademus's room. Nobody comes back.
