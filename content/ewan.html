EWAN [ae'vann] was a telnet and MUD client for Windows that many NannyMUD players used in the 1990s. Its latest version was 1.052.

Ok, how do I get this great program? In Europe, you should be able to get the latest version of the emulator (1.052) from Lysator (256032 bytes). In the USA and Canada, from The Consummate Winsock Apps List: Ewan for the PC ver 1.052 (252802 bytes).
